A little help in the cave

Collect the diamonds, avoid falling rocks and creatures, and find your way out.

Press any key through the title and original instructions. At the menu, select PLAY. Use ↑ / ↓ to choose the starting cave, then press Enter.

← ↑ ↓ →Move / navigate menus
Enter SpaceSelect / continue
EscCancel an original prompt
DeleteGive up a life and retry
F2Pause / resume

The newest held key takes priority. You can press Right while holding Down to turn. Releasing the newest key returns to an older key only if you are still holding it.

KEYBOARD changes the original movement keys. JOYSTICK configures joystick buttons; a connected gamepad’s D-pad or left stick is supported.

EDIT opens the original cave editor. Enter a cave number from 00 to 75, move with the arrows, and use 0–7 for tiles. Enter keeps your changes for this session.

Cassette writes requested by the game download as BOLDERDASH-EDIT.BIN.

Sound starts after your first interaction. The game pauses when you switch away. This page works offline; nothing is uploaded.
